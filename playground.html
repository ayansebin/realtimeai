03 / HOW

What you are looking at.

Real models, zero-shot

Granite TTM r3, FlowState r1.1, PatchTST-FM r2 and TSPulse r1, the open weights on Hugging Face, run through the granite-tsfm library on a CPU runner. No training and no tuning on these datasets.

An honest holdout

Each forecast origin sees only the past. The actual values drawn after the origin were held out. MASE compares the error with a seasonal naive forecast over the same context; coverage checks the band against what really happened.

Open data

Public datasets are CC BY 4.0 or public domain and are credited below. Synthetic datasets are generated with a fixed seed and carry labeled events, so detection can be scored against ground truth.
