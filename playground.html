How it works, sources and licenses
  • Zero-shot. TTM r3, FlowState r1.1, PatchTST-FM r2 and TSPulse r1, open weights, no training.
  • Honest test. The model sees only the past. What happened next was held back.
  • Open data. Real streams are open licensed. Synthetic ones carry known events.
  • Your data. TTM r3 runs in this browser with ONNX Runtime Web, its weights stored at half precision. Anomalies are points far outside its forecast range. Your file is never uploaded.
